Dr. Sachin Vasant Chaudhari is an Associate Professor in the Department of Electronics and Computer Engineering at SRES' Sanjivani College of Engineering, Kopargaon. He joined the institute on July 21, 2010, and possesses over two decades of professional experience, including 19 years in teaching and 1.5 years in industry. His educational background includes a Bachelor of Engineering in Electronics (First Class) from NMU, Jalgaon in 2004, a Master of Engineering in Electronics (First Class) from SPPU, Pune in 2013, and a Ph.D. in Electronics Engineering from Shri Jagdishprasad Jhabarmal Tibrewala University, Chudela, Jhunjhunu in 2018. Dr. Chaudhari can be reached via email at chaudharisachinece@sanjivani.org.in or by phone at 9527740879.

An active researcher, Dr. Chaudhari serves as a recognized Ph.D. guide at SPPU, Pune in the fields of Wireless Communication, PLC and Automation, and Devices and Circuits, currently mentoring three doctoral research scholars. His extensive research portfolio includes dozens of publications in Scopus- and Web of Science-indexed international journals and conference proceedings, alongside several book chapters published by Elsevier and IGI Global. His scholarly work spans antenna design (such as substrate-integrated waveguide antennas), deep learning applications in biomedical imaging and healthcare diagnostics, smart energy systems, gait recognition, and agricultural automation. Furthermore, he has authored textbooks including Bio-Medical Electronics and Instrumentation and Electronic Devices and Circuits, and holds several design and utility patents covering smart heating/cooling systems, medical packaging, projection technology, and IoT-enabled healthcare monitoring. His academic profiles are tracked across Google Scholar, Scopus, ORCID, and Web of Science.

In recognition of his professional contributions, Dr. Chaudhari received the Distinguished Engineer Award from IRDO in 2018, as well as a Best Paper Award at the 6th International Conference on Engineering and Technology (ICET-2025). He maintains professional memberships with the Institution of Engineers (MIE-161046-5) and the International Association of Engineers (IAENG). In academic service, he serves on the editorial board of the American Journal of Science, Engineering and Technology (2025–2028), reviews for international conferences, and has contributed to course development committees. In addition, he regularly participates in and coordinates national faculty development programs, workshops, and seminars covering emerging domains such as AI-assisted scientific writing, National Education Policy (NEP-2020) implementation, data science, and modern engineering pedagogies.
