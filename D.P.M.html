Dr. Dipak Pralhad Mahurkar is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Electronics and Computer Engineering at SRES' Sanjivani College of Engineering, Kopargaon, an autonomous institute affiliated with SPPU, Pune. Having joined the institution on September 1, 2002, he possesses 24 years of teaching experience alongside one year of industry experience. Dr. Mahurkar earned his undergraduate degree with a specialization in Optical Fiber (First Class) from NMU, Jalgaon in 2000, followed by a postgraduate degree in Communication (First Class) from Dr. BAMU, Aurangabad in 2010. In March 2024, he completed his Ph.D. specializing in Image Processing from Oriental University, Madhya Pradesh. For academic and professional communication, he can be contacted at mahurkardipakece@sanjivani.org.in or by phone at 9767734033.

His research expertise centers on image processing, leaf contour feature extraction, species identification, and deep learning architectures, including lightweight cascaded DCNNs for hyperspectral vegetation detection. He has contributed to publications and book series such as Springer's Lecture Notes in Networks and Systems (LNNS), International Journal of Computer Information Systems and Industrial Management Applications, Romanian Journal of Information Technology and Automatic Control, Elsevier's e-Prime – Advances in Electrical Engineering, Electronics and Energy, and the International Journal of Electronics and Communication Engineering. Additionally, he has presented work at forums including the 5th International Conference on Advance Trends in Engineering, Technology and Research (ICATETR-2015) and the 5th International Conference on Intelligent Sustainable Systems (2022). His scholarly work is indexed on Google Scholar, Scopus, and ORCID.

Beyond publishing, Dr. Mahurkar has filed two patents and holds a professional membership with the Indian Society for Technical Education (ISTE: 50008). He actively engages in professional development, having participated in various faculty development programs and workshops covering subjects such as Cloud Computing (Wipro TalentNext), AI-Assisted Scientific Writing, Natural Language Processing and Computer Vision, Technology Management, Data Science and Analytics (ATAL Academy), and NBA Accreditation and Teaching-Learning in Engineering via NPTEL. He also maintains a presence on professional platforms including LinkedIn and YouTube.
