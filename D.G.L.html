Dr. Devidas Gopal Lokhande is an academic faculty member serving at Sanjivani College of Engineering, Kopargaon, within the Department of Electronics and Telecommunication Engineering, where he also fulfills administrative responsibilities such as Deputy Controller of Examinations. Having earned his Master of Engineering (M.E.) in Electronics from Shivaji University, Kolhapur, and pursued advanced research toward a Ph.D., he brings a strong technical foundation to his academic role. His primary research interests and scholarly publications focus on areas including mobile wireless communications, ad-hoc networks (MANETs), the Internet of Things (IoT), and network communication systems, contributing significantly to both student education and the institution's technical activities.
