About Our Project

MY ECE FACULTY

MY ECE FACULTY is a simple and informative website developed to provide students with easy access to information about the faculty members of the Electronics and Computer Engineering department.

The main idea behind this project is to create a common platform where students can explore faculty profiles, learn about their academic roles and find relevant information without having to search through different sources.

Each faculty member has an individual profile page containing their information. Students can explore these profiles through the faculty cards available on the home page.

Our Objectives

Easy Access

Make faculty information easily accessible to students through a single platform.

Better Communication

Help students become familiar with their department's faculty members.

Organized Information

Present faculty details in a structured and easy-to-understand format.

Project Features

01. Faculty Profiles

Individual profile pages for faculty members with their available academic information.

02. Faculty Image Gallery

A visual collection of faculty photographs displayed through a scrolling slideshow.

03. Explore Option

Students can open individual faculty pages using the Explore button.

04. Feedback Facility

Students can share their feedback through the feedback form provided on the website.

05. Simple Interface

A clean and easy-to-use interface that helps students navigate the website.

Technologies Used

HTML5

Used to structure the website and organize its content.

CSS3

Used for styling, layouts, animations, cards and responsive design.

VS Code

Used as the development environment for writing and managing project files.

GitHub Pages

Used to host and publish the website online.
